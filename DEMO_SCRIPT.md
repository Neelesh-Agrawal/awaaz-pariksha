# Demo video: script and shot list (under 3 minutes)

Record in Chrome at 1280×720 or larger, with system sound and your microphone both captured (OBS, or Windows Game Bar with "record audio"). Speak to the app the way a student would: naturally, not slowly. Do one practice run first, then reset with **More → Start over** before the real take.

Keep the app's voice audible. The judges need to hear the app talk back, because that is the product.

Before you start:
- Chrome, internet on, microphone allowed for `localhost:8080`.
- Close other tabs and turn off notifications.
- Zoom the page to 110% so text is readable on YouTube.
- Use a headset microphone if you have one.

---

## 0:00 – 0:20 · The problem (talking over the home screen)

> "In India, a blind student writes an exam by dictating to a scribe. Finding one is hard, they cost families money, and the student can't check what was actually written. Awaaz Pariksha lets the student hear the paper, answer by voice, and check and fix every word themselves."

**On screen:** home card with the paper details.

## 0:20 – 0:35 · Start, eyes closed

Press any key. Then say:
- "Hindi" → the app switches language and answers in Hindi. Then say "English" to switch back. (Shows languages in 10 seconds; skip it if it runs long.)
- "Start exam."

**Show:** the status strip changes colour. The app gives the question count and the one rule: tap, Space, or say "Awaaz".

## 0:35 – 1:15 · A written answer

The app reads Question 1. Recording starts by itself. Dictate two or three sentences, including a mixed-language one:

> "Biogas is made from cow dung and kitchen waste. Gaon mein log isko khana banane ke liye use karte hain. It reduces air pollution."

Then press **Space** and say "read it back".

**Show:** words appearing on the ruled lines while you speak, the voice orb moving, and the app reading the answer back.

## 1:15 – 1:45 · Fix one word without counting

Press Space: "change biogas to biofuel."

The app reads the changed sentence. Then press Space and say "undo", and the app says it is back.

Press Space again: "delete waste."

> Voice-over: "No sentence numbers, no word numbers. You tell it what to change, the way you'd tell a scribe."

## 1:45 – 2:15 · Multiple choice: pick, then lock

Say "next question" until you reach a multiple-choice question (or say "go to question 6").
- "Read the options."
- "I think it's Mercury." → "You picked B, Mercury. Not locked yet." **Show** the dashed amber outline.
- "Lock it." **Show** the filled green row.
- "Actually make it C." → the app says the new pick is not locked.

## 2:15 – 2:35 · Nothing is lost

Reload the page (F5). Open the same question.

> Voice-over: "Every answer, pick and lock is saved as it happens."

**Show:** the answers and the locked option are back.

## 2:35 – 2:55 · Submit and the record

Say "submit". The app summarises answered, empty and unlocked questions, then asks "Submit now? Yes or no." Say "yes".

**Show:** the answer sheet with the Record line, which shows what was dictated, which voice edits were made, and the times.

> "The student's words are kept exactly. The app never writes or improves an answer. It runs in a browser, needs no account, and the teacher can upload any question paper as a PDF."

## 2:55 – 3:00 · Close

> "Awaaz Pariksha. Your voice, your answers."

**On screen:** the GitHub link.

---

## If something goes wrong while recording

- If a command is misheard, keep going and say it another way. It is honest, and it shows recovery. Only re-record if the app gets stuck.
- If the app speaks over you, press **Esc** to stop it speaking.
- Uploading a question paper (teacher side) is optional. If you have time, add a 15-second cut of **Teacher setup → Upload** with `samples/sample-paper.pdf`, and cut the language switch to make room.
